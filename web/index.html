    Unicode inspection

      NFC/NFD change Unicode representation; NFKC/NFKD can also replace compatibility characters. Case transforms are mechanical and may alter names or acronyms. Clean spacing preserves ordinary paragraph breaks, but may change indentation. Preview and compare before using the result.

      A suggestion is a starting point.

      Meaning, not a verdict

      Datamuse draws on dictionaries, phonetic data and text corpora. A related word may have a different tone or sense. Results keep the provider’s ordering; scores are not a percentage of similarity and are not compared across searches. Syllables and word classes can be ambiguous.

      Your words and privacy

      Submitted words, patterns and topic hints go to Datamuse, directly or through the configured Word Weave service. That service may cache query results for one hour. Local filters, comparisons, shortlists and the entire Text utilities workspace stay in this tab. Datamuse says query logs are discarded at the end of the following day. Avoid sending text you want to keep private.

      Keep exploring

      Searches are manual, with a short cooldown and tab-local cache. Results can change as the provider updates its sources. Save a receipt to keep a returned list and its retrieval time; compare only the captured word sets.
