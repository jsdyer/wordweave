    Meaning, not a verdict

    Datamuse draws on dictionaries, phonetic data and text corpora. A related word may have a different tone or sense. Results keep the provider’s ordering; scores are not a percentage of similarity and are not compared across searches. Syllables and word classes can be ambiguous.

    Your words and privacy

    Submitted words, patterns and topic hints go to Datamuse. Local filters, comparisons and shortlists stay in this tab. Datamuse says query logs are discarded at the end of the following day. Avoid sending text you want to keep private.

    Keep exploring

    Searches are manual, with a short cooldown and tab-local cache. No account or key is needed for current access. Datamuse has announced API keys will be required from 1 February 2027; the owner can connect a configured server endpoint when a key is issued.
